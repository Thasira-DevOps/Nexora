            Notifications

            Nexora can alert you about reminders, event start times and finished focus sessions. This works while the app is open, even in the background. Nothing is sent to any server, so a fully closed app cannot wake your phone. For reminders in that case, tap "Add to phone" on a note or event to put it in your phone's own calendar.
